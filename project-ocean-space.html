01Context & Problem

A fish business selling online needed a store where customers can browse products, order, and pay digitally, while the owner manages products, incoming orders, stock purchasing, and in-store sales in the same system.

02Scope

  • Customer: catalogue, product detail, cart, buy now, checkout, Midtrans payment, order history, invoice download.
  • Admin: product management, order status updates, purchasing, cashier (in-store sales), attendance, and fees.

04Payment Flow

Checkout creates an order, the customer pays through Midtrans, and Midtrans sends a notification that updates the order:

Midtrans statusPayment statusOrder status
settlementpaidpending (ready to process)
cancel / expire / denyfailedcancelled

05Looking Back

Working on payment systems since then, I would design the notification handling more defensively:

  • Verify the notification signature before trusting it.
  • Make the update idempotent so a repeated notification cannot change an order twice.
  • Check the transaction status with the provider when a notification does not arrive.

These are the same principles I applied in the payment integration case study.
