Case Study
Risk Management · Azra Hospital

Risk Register

Turning the hospital's spreadsheet-based risk register into a system that follows the full risk cycle: identification, analysis, treatment planning, and evaluation.

RoleSystem design & fullstack development
ContextInternship, Azra Hospital (Nov 2025 – May 2026)
TechLaravel, MySQL, PHP
DeliverablesProcess mapping from spreadsheet, database design, application, PDF reports

01Context & Problem

Each unit kept its risk register in a large spreadsheet with many columns: activity, objective, risk statement, cause, existing controls, probability, impact, risk level, treatment plan, schedule, owner, and person in charge of follow-up.

My task was to translate that spreadsheet into a structured system: separate the steps that happen at different times, keep the scoring consistent across units, and produce the same register as a report.

02Analysis: from spreadsheet to process

The spreadsheet mixes four activities that are done at different moments. I split them into four stages, each with its own screen and table:

StageWhat is recordedOutput
1. IdentificationActivity, objective, risk statement, cause, impact, category, scope, controllable / uncontrollableRisk with a code, per period
2. AnalysisExisting control (description, design, effectiveness), probability, impactRisk score and rank
3. Adequacy & treatmentTreatment plan, schedule, person in chargeAction plan
4. EvaluationResidual probability and impact, incidentsResidual score, % risk reduction

03Key Requirements

  • Consistent scoring: probability and impact come from master tables (value, colour, impact description per area), so every unit scores risk the same way.
  • Risk library: units can copy common risks from a library instead of typing them again.
  • Periods: risks belong to a period and quarter; reporting frequency is recorded per risk.
  • Role-based access per menu, managed by an administrator.
  • Reports: risk register, adequacy analysis, evaluation, and a single-risk profile exported to PDF.

04Design

Scoring

probability×impact=risk score → rank

After treatment, the same calculation with residual probability and impact gives the residual score, and the system shows the percentage of risk reduction.
