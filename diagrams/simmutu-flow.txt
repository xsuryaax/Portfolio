title Quality Indicator Reporting & PDSA Follow-up (SIMMUTU)

actor "Unit Staff" as U
participant "SIMMUTU" as S
database "db simmutu" as DB
actor "Validator" as V
actor "Quality Team" as Q

U->S: input numerator & denominator per indicator
note over S: calculate value, compare with\ntarget (higher, lower, or range)
S->DB: save report (achieved / not achieved)
V->S: review unit report
S->DB: save validator value & status (valid / not valid)
Q->S: analyse indicator per period
S->DB: save analysis & follow-up
alt indicator not achieved
Q->S: assign PDSA to unit (per quarter)
S->DB: PDSA status: assigned
U->S: submit Plan-Do-Study-Act
S->DB: status: submitted
opt revision needed
Q->S: request revision
S->DB: status: revised
U->S: resubmit PDSA
end
Q->S: approve PDSA
S->DB: status: approved
end
