title Hospital Risk Register Cycle

actor "Unit" as U
participant "Risk Register" as R
database "db risk" as DB
actor "Quality Team" as Q

U->R: identify risk (activity, cause, impact, category)
note over R: or copy from risk library
R->DB: save risk with code & period
U->R: analyse existing control, probability, impact
note over R: score = probability x impact\n-> risk rank
R->DB: save score & rank
U->R: plan treatment (action, schedule, PIC)
R->DB: save treatment plan
U->R: evaluate residual probability & impact
note over R: residual score,\n% risk reduction
R->DB: save evaluation
Q->R: view risk register, export PDF
R-->Q: risk register report
