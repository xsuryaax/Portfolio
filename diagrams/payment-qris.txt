title Pembayaran Checkout QRIS (Konsep)

actor "Pembeli" as B
participant "Marketplace App" as APP
participant "Payment Service" as PS
database "db payment" as DB
participant "Payment Gateway" as PG

B->APP: checkout pesanan
APP->PS: req create payment (QRIS)
PS->DB: simpan transaksi (PENDING)
PS->PG: req generate QR
PG-->PS: res QR string + batas waktu
PS-->APP: res QR + batas waktu
APP-->B: tampilkan QR
B->PG: scan & bayar (e-wallet / m-banking)
PG->PS: callback hasil pembayaran
note over PS: verifikasi signature,\ntolak callback ganda (idempotent)
PS->DB: update transaksi (PAID)
PS-->PG: res ack
PS->APP: notifikasi pesanan dibayar
APP-->B: tampilkan pembayaran berhasil
alt callback tidak datang sampai batas waktu
APP->PS: req cek status
PS->PG: req inquiry status
PG-->PS: res status
PS->DB: update transaksi (PAID / EXPIRED)
PS-->APP: res status akhir
end
