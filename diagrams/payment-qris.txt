title QRIS Checkout Payment (Concept)

actor "Buyer" as B
participant "Marketplace App" as APP
participant "Payment Service" as PS
database "db payment" as DB
participant "Payment Gateway" as PG

B->APP: checkout order
APP->PS: req create payment (QRIS)
PS->DB: save transaction (PENDING)
PS->PG: req generate QR
PG-->PS: res QR string + expiry
PS-->APP: res QR + expiry
APP-->B: show QR
B->PG: scan & pay (e-wallet / m-banking)
PG->PS: callback payment result
note over PS: verify signature,\nreject duplicate callback (idempotent)
PS->DB: update transaction (PAID)
PS-->PG: res ack
PS->APP: notify order paid
APP-->B: show payment success
alt no callback before expiry
APP->PS: req check status
PS->PG: req inquiry status
PG-->PS: res status
PS->DB: update transaction (PAID / EXPIRED)
PS-->APP: res final status
end
