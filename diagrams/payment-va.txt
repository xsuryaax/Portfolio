title Virtual Account Payment (Concept)

actor "Buyer" as B
participant "Marketplace App" as APP
participant "Payment Service" as PS
database "db payment" as DB
participant "Bank" as BK

B->APP: choose Virtual Account
APP->PS: req create payment (VA)
PS->DB: save transaction (PENDING)
PS->BK: req create VA number
BK-->PS: res VA number
PS-->APP: res VA number + expiry
APP-->B: show VA number
B->BK: transfer to VA
BK->PS: req payment notification
note over PS: check VA exists, amount matches,\nnot expired, not already paid
PS->DB: update transaction (PAID)
PS-->BK: res success
PS->APP: notify order paid
APP-->B: show payment success
