title Pembayaran Virtual Account (Konsep)

actor "Pembeli" as B
participant "Marketplace App" as APP
participant "Payment Service" as PS
database "db payment" as DB
participant "Bank" as BK

B->APP: pilih Virtual Account
APP->PS: req create payment (VA)
PS->DB: simpan transaksi (PENDING)
PS->BK: req buat nomor VA
BK-->PS: res nomor VA
PS-->APP: res nomor VA + batas waktu
APP-->B: tampilkan nomor VA
B->BK: transfer ke VA
BK->PS: req notifikasi pembayaran
note over PS: cek VA ada, nominal sesuai,\nbelum kedaluwarsa, belum dibayar
PS->DB: update transaksi (PAID)
PS-->BK: res sukses
PS->APP: notifikasi pesanan dibayar
APP-->B: tampilkan pembayaran berhasil
